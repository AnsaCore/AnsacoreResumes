Glue vs EMR vs Kubernetes
June 2022
As someone who enjoys PySpark, I thought it to be interesting to have a look at AWS Glue and compare it to possible alternatives such as EMR and Kubernetes. Glue was a true delight to work with since it immediately worked. Kubernetes is a tough beast to tame to say the least. Setup and maintenance costs are definitely higher than Glue. I then studied the cost differences between EMR, Kubernetes and Glue. I quickly found that the operational cost of Glue is higher than the operational costs of EMR and K8s as soon as you exceed 500 hours of computations on a reference VM. Take into account the maintenance cost of K8s and you would need hundred thousands of hours before Kubernetes would be cheaper to set up. Even though operational costs of Glue seem higher, it is really lower when you take everything into account! Another important fact is that companies would have more manpower available for value creation instead of wasting it all on creating and maintaining a Kubernetes cluster for their Big Data Engineering projects.
